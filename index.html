Our story

A taste of home

Dastarkhwan was created around a simple idea — good food brings people together. Inspired by the traditional Pakistani dastarkhwan, our kitchen combines time-honored recipes, fresh ingredients, and the warmth of home cooking.

Every dish leaves the kitchen the way it would in a Lahori home — unhurried, seasoned by hand, and meant to be shared.
